## Our goal

We aim for this website and our customer portal to conform with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. We have not yet completed an independent accessibility audit, so we do not claim full conformance.

## Scope

This statement covers crestmontwebsitedevelopment.com and the Crestmont customer portal. It does not cover websites we build for our customers. Their accessibility is described in each customer's agreement with us (Master Services Agreement, Section 14.5).

## What we do

- Semantic HTML with headings, landmarks and a skip link.
- Keyboard-operable navigation, forms and dialogs with visible focus.
- Form errors announced to assistive technology and linked to their fields.
- Colour contrast checked against WCAG AA for text.
- Respect for reduced-motion preferences.
- Core pages and forms are designed to work without JavaScript. **[VERIFY: the published text says "All features work without JavaScript". Keep that wording only if it is confirmed for every current page and portal feature.]**

## Known limitations

- Some third-party services we link to, such as payment pages, are operated by others and may have their own accessibility limitations.
- Documents and files uploaded by customers or third parties may not be accessible.

## Feedback

If you encounter a barrier, email support@crestmontwebsitedevelopment.com or use the contact form. Tell us the page and the problem. We will respond and work on a fix, or on another way to get what you need.
