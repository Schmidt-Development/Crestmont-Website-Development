## 1. Who We Are

This policy explains how Talent Revenue Systems LLC, trading as Crestmont Website Development ("**Crestmont**", "**we**", "**us**"), handles Personal Data collected through crestmontwebsitedevelopment.com, the customer portal (the "**Portal**"), telephone calls, our sales activities and our Services. We operate online. Our business mailing address is 1409 Lake Lansing Rd, Lansing, MI 48912. Privacy questions can be sent to legal@crestmontwebsitedevelopment.com.

Defined terms (**Customer**, **Services**, **Customer Data**, **Personal Data**, **AI Features**, **AI Receptionist**, **Subprocessor**, **Security Incident**) have the same meaning as in our Master Services Agreement.

**Scope.** This policy covers Personal Data that Crestmont controls: data about visitors to our website, people who contact us or whom we call, people whose business contact details we collect to find potential customers, and our Customers and their staff who use the Portal. It does **not** cover Customer Data that we process for a Customer, for example submissions to a website we host for a Customer. That data is governed by the Customer's own privacy notice and our Data Processing Addendum with that Customer.

### 3A. Finding and contacting potential customers

We look for businesses that may need a website, using information that businesses publish about themselves, the Overture Maps and Common Crawl open datasets, and the businesses' own public websites. We keep the business contact details we find in our customer-relationship records. We call a person only as the law allows. Today we place automated or AI-voice calls only to people who have asked us to call them back, at the time they asked for. We do not currently make unsolicited sales calls with automated or AI voice agents. If that changes, we will do so only with the consent the law requires, and we will update this policy first. You can ask us at any time to stop contacting you or to delete your details (Section 11). We keep a record of a request not to be called so that we can honour it.

## 6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use:
- **Strictly necessary cookies** for sign-in sessions, security (CSRF protection) and short-lived notices.
- **One first-party attribution cookie (`cm_attr`)**, which lasts up to 30 days. It records three things: the campaign (UTM) parameters in the link you followed; the referring page, reduced to its address without query string; and the page on our site where you first arrived. We use this so that a quote request can be credited to that source. It is HttpOnly, is read only by our server, and is never shared with advertisers.

We do **not** use third-party analytics or advertising cookies. If that changes, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required.

## 7. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a Do Not Track (DNT) or Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we do not record the referring page in `cm_attr`. Campaign parameters in the link itself and your landing page on our site are still recorded. Because we do not sell or share Personal Data for advertising, there is no further sale or sharing to opt out of. **[COUNSEL: Whether recording campaign parameters despite a GPC signal is acceptable, or whether the cookie should be skipped entirely when GPC is present.]**

## 8. Phone Calls, Recordings and Automated Voice Agents

- Calls to and from Crestmont may be answered or placed by an automated voice agent that uses AI, or by our staff.
- Our automated agents never claim to be human. If you ask whether you are speaking with an automated system, you will be told truthfully. Where the law requires us to tell you at the start of a call that you are speaking with an automated or artificial-voice system, or to identify Crestmont, we do so.
- Where the law requires consent or notice before a call is recorded or transcribed, we record or transcribe only after that consent is given or that notice is provided. We do not treat silence, or your not asking, as consent. If a required consent is not given, we keep no recording, transcript or summary of the call. We keep only the call record (number, time, outcome) and the details you ask us to act on, such as an appointment or a callback request.
- To operate a call, our telephony and voice providers process the call audio as it happens.
- We call you back with an automated agent only when you have asked us to, at the time arranged. You can tell us during any call, or by email, not to call you again. We record that request and honour it.
- Our automated agents can give published prices, book appointments and callbacks, and pass matters to a person. They do not approve refunds, accept contracts or change prices.

## 12. Children

Our website and Services are intended for adults; you must be at least 18 to buy Services. ## 13. Security
