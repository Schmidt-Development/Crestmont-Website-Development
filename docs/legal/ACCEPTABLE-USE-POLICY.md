## 1. Scope

This Acceptable Use Policy ("**AUP**") applies to everyone who uses the Platform, to every Customer, and to all websites, Deliverables, AI Features and AI Receptionist lines that Crestmont builds, hosts or maintains. It forms part of the Website Terms of Use and the Master Services Agreement. Defined terms have the meanings given in the Master Services Agreement. Customers are responsible for their users' and website visitors' compliance where they control it.

## 2. Prohibited Content

You may not use the Services or Platform to create, host, publish or transmit content that:
(a) is unlawful, or promotes illegal goods, services or activity;
(b) infringes intellectual property, privacy or publicity rights;
(c) sexually exploits or endangers minors (we report any such material to the authorities);
(d) harasses, threatens, defames or incites violence or discrimination against people;
(e) is deceptive, including fake reviews or testimonials, impersonation of a person or organisation, misleading pricing, or undisclosed paid endorsements;
(f) contains or links to malware, or is designed for phishing or credential harvesting; or
(g) makes regulated claims (for example health, financial or legal claims) without the required substantiation, licences or disclosures.

**Higher-risk businesses.** Crestmont does not exclude lawful industries as a category. Crestmont reviews each business in a higher-risk category individually before accepting an Order, for example adult content, gambling, firearms, cannabis, debt collection or high-risk financial products. It may decline, or set conditions on, work that the law, its payment processor or its infrastructure providers restrict.

## 3. Prohibited Conduct

You may not:
(a) access accounts, data or systems without authorisation, or share credentials;
(b) probe, scan, penetration-test or load-test the Platform or any hosted website **without Crestmont's prior written permission**. This includes vulnerability scanning, fuzzing and denial-of-service testing. Good-faith security reports are welcome at legal@crestmontwebsitedevelopment.com. **[CONFIRM: a vulnerability-disclosure policy and security.txt file go live once a dedicated security mailbox is set up and verified (Founder decision FD-41). Decide whether to include a safe-harbour statement.]**
(c) bypass rate limits, access controls, tenant isolation or malware-scan warnings;
(d) upload files you know or suspect to be malicious (Portal files may not have been scanned for malware);
(e) use the Services in a way that degrades them for others, or exceed plan resource limits deliberately; or
(f) scrape or harvest data from the Platform.

## 4. Email, Messaging and Telemarketing

(a) No unsolicited bulk email, and no email that breaches the CAN-SPAM Act or similar laws.
(b) No text messages or calls that breach the Telephone Consumer Protection Act, the Telemarketing Sales Rule, Do-Not-Call rules or state equivalents.
(c) **An AI Receptionist on a Customer's line is inbound-only.** Using it, or any Crestmont-built tool, to place outbound automated, artificial-voice or AI-voice calls or texts is prohibited unless new written terms are in place and the prior express consent the law requires, in writing where required, has been obtained and documented (MSA Schedule C.7).
(d) Website forms that Crestmont builds must not be used to collect consent for marketing that the Customer cannot document.

## 5. AI Misuse

You may not use AI Features or the AI Receptionist to:
(a) generate content described in Section 2;
(b) impersonate a real person, claim falsely that an AI agent is human, or leave out a disclosure that the law requires (for example that a caller is speaking with an automated or artificial-voice system);
(c) record or transcribe calls without the consent required by law;
(d) make or present automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on individuals, for example decisions on credit, employment, housing or insurance; or
(e) attempt to extract another Customer's data, system prompts or credentials, or to override the AI Features' safeguards.

## 6. Enforcement

6.1 We may investigate suspected breaches. We may: decline or stop work; remove or disable content; suspend hosting, Portal access or the AI Receptionist; or terminate under the Master Services Agreement.

6.2 We give notice and a chance to fix the problem first where practical. We may act immediately for illegal content, malware or phishing, active security threats, conduct that harms others, or where the law requires it.

6.3 We will tell you what action we took and why, unless the law prohibits it. Suspension does not remove your ownership of Customer Content. Data export follows the Master Services Agreement. **[COUNSEL: DMCA designated-agent registration and a takedown/counter-notice process for hosted Customer websites; Section 230 considerations.]**

6.4 We may report unlawful activity to the relevant authorities and cooperate with lawful requests.
