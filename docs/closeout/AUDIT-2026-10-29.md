## A. Executive verdict

**Launch recommendation: NO-GO.** Crestmont has an unusually complete, well-tested codebase — 567 local tests pass, 32 Owner modules run on real data, and no mock or fake-success screens were found — but it cannot take a real customer today. One critical security defect (CREST-GAP-001), 17 launch blockers, and an undeployed production environment stand in the way. The longest pole is not engineering: it is the legal entity, attorney review of the agreements, domain control, and the Stripe and Resend accounts, all Founder-owned.
